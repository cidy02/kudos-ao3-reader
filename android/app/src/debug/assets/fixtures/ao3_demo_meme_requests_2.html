  • The orchard beyond the timetable by EveningInk (AO3_Reader)

    Moonlit Postal Service - Original Fiction
    • Travel
    • A Promise Kept Across Seasons

    A courier follows a route that is missing from every printed timetable. The first stop is an orchard whose trees carry tiny paper envelopes instead of fruit. Each envelope holds a memory someone has chosen to give away, and each branch is marked with a destination that no longer exists. Write about the person who tends the orchard and the courier who arrives with an undeliverable letter. Let the story take its time with the ordinary work: checking the weather, making tea, mending the satchel, and deciding which path is safe after the rain. The letter need not reveal a secret or save the world. It might be an invitation to dinner, an apology for a forgotten birthday, or a description of a garden. What matters is that someone has kept it dry through many seasons and still believes there is a door where it belongs. End with that door opening and the smell of warm bread.
