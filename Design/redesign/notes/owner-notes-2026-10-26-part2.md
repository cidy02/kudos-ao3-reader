# Owner notes, part 2: the wash and the app background (2026-10-26)

This adds to `kudos-owner-notes.md`. Apply both points to the direction board (Step 0) and to the rule sweep
(item 6d). If the direction board is already published, revise it to follow these.

## 1. Keep the fandom wash, but only on a work's or a fandom's own page

Today nearly every screen is washed in some colour: Work Detail is green, Library is pink from the app's red,
Home is peach from the work being read, and Account and the writing forms are tinted too. Because it's on every
screen, the wash means nothing, and it adds to everything feeling equally loud.

Apple uses a wash only on pages about one piece of content, with the colour taken from its artwork: a Podcasts
show page, a book in Apple Books. List and browse screens use the plain system background.

Rule: ask "whose page is this?"

- **A work's or a fandom's** (Work Detail, a fandom's works list, possibly Author and Series): keep the wash.
  It does the job of a cover and tells you whose page you're on.
- **The reader's or the app's** (Home, Library, Account, queues, Settings, editors, sheets): no wash. On Home,
  the Continue Reading card carries the fandom colour, not the page.

Two rules go with it:

- The wash colours the background only. Buttons, the tab bar and toolbars use one app colour. That also fixes
  Home's + button turning green after you visit a green work (evidence 18).
- The wash fades out as you scroll, and the large title shrinks into the navigation bar above it.

The direction board can still show this as an option next to "wash everywhere", so I can compare them.

## 2. No custom app background. Use the system's two backgrounds per screen type

Don't add a texture, paper colour or gradient behind the app. It looks home-made, it makes Liquid Glass harder
to read, and it would need a version for each of the four themes.

Use Apple's two standard backgrounds, chosen by type of screen:

- **Plain** (white in light mode, black in dark) for content screens: Home, Library shelves, Work Detail. The
  fandom-coloured cards are the raised layer on it.
- **Grouped** (light grey in light mode, near-black in dark) for lists and settings: Account, Settings, the queue
  list, forms. Rounded white sections sit on it, like the Settings app.

Every screen then has two clear levels: a canvas and one kind of surface on it. Today's boxes sit on tinted
backgrounds with fills chosen screen by screen, so nothing reads as in front of anything else. Fixing that is
most of the cure for "one-dimensional". Plain won't look bland: the cards, rings and four-tag symbols stand out
more on a calm canvas, the way covers do in Apple Books.

The Sepia theme already gives the whole app a paper tone (`shots/iphone-variants/home_sepia.jpg`). That is the
right place for warmth: a choice the reader makes, not the default. Give Sepia the same two levels, a paper
canvas with slightly lighter surfaces, and do the same for Dark and OLED in item 9.
