# What Kudos is missing — critique panel (2026-10-26)

**What Kudos is missing**

**The short answer**

The redesign built good parts. Three things stop it from ever feeling done:

1. **The app draws its own navigation instead of letting iOS do it.** The small behaviours that make Apple's apps feel finished never happen: titles that shrink into the bar, text that fades under floating buttons, a tab bar that stays put.
2. **The parts have no rules.** Colour, boxes, edges, labels and buttons are chosen screen by screen. Nothing leads, and the same thing looks different in each place.
3. **The app hides what it knows about your reading.** It tracks progress, kudos, new chapters and hours read, but shows little of it. The moment the app is named after, finishing a fic and leaving kudos, passes with nothing on screen.

**Root causes, ranked**

**1. Home-made navigation instead of the system's (effort L)**
- **What you see:**
  - In 17, "Winter Garden" slides under the back circle and the status bar, and no small title replaces it.
  - In 03, there are two stacked back buttons plus a "HOME › QUEUES" breadcrumb.
  - In 06, reader text shows through the title capsule and is cut by its edge.
  - In 08, the fourth onboarding point is chopped off by the footer.
  - Work Detail (16) has no tab bar, but 02 and 03 keep it.
  - The wide Library (11) has two sidebar buttons.
- **Why it nags:** these collisions show up the moment you scroll.
- **What to change:**
  - Let `NavigationStack` own the bar: `.navigationTitle`, `.navigationSubtitle` for the author, buttons as `ToolbarItem`s, and `.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .top)`.
  - Remove `hidesSystemNavigationBar`, the hand-built back swipe and the glass back circles.
  - Keep the fandom wash as the background and the fandom kicker as the first row. Drop the kickers that aren't AO3 information (LIBRARY, HOME › QUEUES, EDIT WORK).
  - Reader: attach the top bar and position card with `.safeAreaBar(edge:)`, or `UIScrollEdgeElementContainerInteraction` on Readium's scroll view, and group the three shapes in one `GlassEffectContainer`.
  - Onboarding: put the footer in `.safeAreaBar(edge: .bottom)`.
  - Keep the tab bar on ordinary pushed pages.
  - Compose: `LargeTopAppBar` with `exitUntilCollapsedScrollBehavior`, the actions in `Scaffold(bottomBar)`, and a fade behind the reader overlays.
- **Design canvas proposal:** collapsing titles, a separate Search button and a floating tab bar cover most of this. Not covered: the reader's edge fade, the double back button, the onboarding footer, the tab bar vanishing on pushes, and the two sidebars.

**2. No rules for colour, shape and type (effort M)**
- **What you see:**
  - In 18, "+" turns green after you visit a Frozen work while the page stays purple.
  - The selected tab is red in 04 and purple in 01 and 02.
  - The Sepia sheets in 12 have purple ticks.
  - The same four AO3 facts are drawn four ways: a 2×2 tray and a 1×4 tray in 01, chips in 02, and a strip in 16. "No warnings" is green in 16 and grey elsewhere.
  - Work Detail (16) uses seven box styles.
  - 02, 03 and 16 have three different left edges. Home (01) has one, and it looks the most finished.
  - 03 has eight all-caps labels.
  - "Resume" is a labelled pill in 01 and a ▶ disc in 16. "Try Again" is bare text in 07.
- **Why it nags:** when everything is tinted, boxed and capitalised, nothing stands out. A colour that depends on the last screen you visited looks like a bug.
- **What to change (five rules):**
  1. **Colour:** the theme accent for all chrome (tab bar, toolbars, sheets, filter pills). The fandom colour goes only into the wash, kicker and cards. Stop `HeroWash`'s `.accentTint` and the window tint from carrying the fandom colour.
  2. **Edges:** one 16pt edge on every screen.
  3. **Surfaces:** two only, a panel and a capsule chip.
  4. **Capitals:** only for the kicker and stat captions. Section headers become `.title3.bold()`, and fixed point sizes become text styles.
  5. **Buttons:** one main style, a `.glassProminent` capsule labelled "Resume", used everywhere. `.glass` for secondary actions.
  - Compose: `colorScheme.primary` for chrome, a separate CompositionLocal for the fandom colours, `shapes.large` panels, and the default chip shape.
- **Design canvas proposal:** the shared 2×2 Symbols block fixes the four AO3 drawings, and plain grouped surfaces fix the box sprawl. Not covered: the tint leak, the edges, the capitals, and the button styles.

**3. Your reading life is invisible (effort M, mostly small pieces)**
- **What you see:**
  - In 06 you are in the last chapter, and the only sign is "0 min left", printed twice.
  - "Mark as Finished" is item 6 of 9 in the long-press menu (14), and the menu has no Kudos.
  - Kudos shows only as a count in 16 and 02, even though the app stores whether you gave it.
  - Library cards (02, 11) show no progress, while Home shows 42% and 71%.
  - Winter Garden has an unread chapter but no badge in 01, and Recently Updated sits below Comfort Reads (18).
  - Work Detail (16) labels "4/6" as COMPLETE, and Library (11) calls the same work Unknown.
  - The queue header (03) gives "10 KB", not reading time, and Up Next has no Resume button.
  - Home has no reading stats, and Reading Insights is filed under Locations in 11.
- **What to change:**
  - At the end of a work, put an end card in the position card's slot: "The End", your reading time, Give Kudos with a heart animation and haptic, Comment, and the next work in the queue. A work in progress says "You're caught up" instead.
  - Kudos: replace the "AO3 / OK" alert with the heart animation, and show a filled heart on cards and Work Detail once given.
  - Library cards: a thin `ProgressView` plus "42% · opened 6 min ago".
  - A "+1 chapter" chip on Continue Reading cards.
  - Work out WIP or Complete from the chapter count in `completionStatus`, so every screen agrees.
  - Queue: "9 h 40 min left" in the header, and a Resume button on Up Next.
  - Home: a three-figure stats strip that links to Reading Insights.
  - Compose: `ElevatedCard` for the end card, `IconToggleButton`, `LinearProgressIndicator`, `Badge`.
- **Design canvas proposal:** covers none of this. Its Read Aloud bar is not a resume or end-of-work moment.

**Quick wins (each under a day)**
- Clip the zoom-back animation to the card's rounded shape. In 18 the card sits on a square plate.
- Fix the status label from the chapter count (16, 11).
- Reader card: show the chapter name, show "<1 min" instead of "0 min", and say time left once (06, 10).
- Reader error page: add Close and Download Again, and hide the raw error under "Details" (15).
- Use one 16pt edge, and align the chip rows to the left (02, 11, 16).
- Stop the stats row ending a line with a dangling "•" (02).
- Group the long-press menu into sections, add Share, and add "…" to items that open a sheet (14).
- Move Recently Updated to right after Continue Reading on Home (18).
- Account: drop shortcut tiles that repeat a row below, and remove the chevrons that do nothing when signed out (09, 04).
- Queue: hide filter pills with a count of 0, and say "offline" once (03).

**What to keep**
- The fandom wash, kicker and per-fandom card colours as identity (01).
- The density: Library cards keep the summary, chips and all seven stats (02).
- Home's single left edge (01). Use it as the template for every screen.
- The progress ring and Resume hero (01), and the "Tap to reveal" blur on mature works.
- Browse's offline page (07): use it as the pattern for every error.
- The system-style sheets with ordinary capitalisation (05, 12), and the queue's segmented progress bar (03).

**Questions for you**
1. Should the fandom colour ever reach buttons, such as the hero's Resume, or stay on identity only, with one app accent everywhere else?
2. Should the reader default to New York (serif) for people who never picked a font?
3. Do you want a "now reading" bar above the tab bar, like Music's mini player? If so, does it share that spot with Read Aloud, and should it hide while Home's hero is on screen?

_Kudos's content is strong, but the app draws its own navigation layer: page names and kickers sit inside the content, glass circles are hand-made, the back swipe is re-implemented, and a fandom tint repaints the tab bar and toolbars. Because of that, none of the iOS 26 system behaviours that make Books, Music and Podcasts feel first-party ever happen: titles that collapse into the bar, the soft scroll-edge effect, one stable accent, a tab bar that stays put and minimises, a "now playing" style accessory. What's missing is the system chrome doing its job: keep the colour and density in the content, with quiet, standard chrome above it._

### reading-soul

_Kudos already records a reader's whole reading life: progress, hours by fandom, kudos given, chapter titles, new WIP chapters, and the line where you stopped. Almost none of it shows where the reader looks, and the app has no climax: finishing a fic silently sets a flag, and leaving kudos (the act the app is named after) ends in a system alert titled "AO3". It is a well-made AO3 client with fandom-tinted cards. What's missing is the moment of finishing a work and thanking the author, and the feeling that the library remembers you._

- **Finishing a work has no moment: no 'The End' and no kudos or comment prompt** (high, M) — On AO3, the end of a work is where the culture happens: the work page ends with the Kudos and Comment buttons, and AO3's own EPUB afterword asks readers to comment. Apple Books shows an end-of-book page and Kindle shows 'Before you go'. In Kudos, `book.onReachedPublicationEnd` (Features/ReaderReadium/ReadiumReaderView.swift ~1396) only sets `work.isFinished = true` and saves. `ReadiumBook.isAtPublicationEnd` is published but no view reads it. The reader already recognises AO3's `.afterword` place (ReaderTimeEstimate.swift `Place`). So the app named after this moment shows nothing at it, and this is the missing hero moment. Fix: SwiftUI: when `onReachedPublicationEnd` fires, or the position place becomes `.afterword`, swap `ReaderPositionCard` for an end card in the same glass slot (`GlassEffectContainer`/`.glassEffect()`), in the work's subject palette. Contents:
- A serif 'The End' with the title, 'by nine_of_wands' and the fandom kicker.
- One line about the reader's own read, e.g. '62,004 words · 3 sessions · 4 h 12 min', taken from the existing `ReadingSession` rows.
- A primary 'Give Kudos' button: `heart` becomes `heart.fill` with `.symbolEffect(.bounce)` and `.sensoryFeedback(.success, trigger:)`.
- Secondary 'Comment' (the existing `commentsSheet`) and 'Bookmark'.
- A 'Next' row: next in the queue (ReadingQueueService) or next in the series.

A WIP ('4/?') instead shows 'You're caught up · Subscribe for chapter 5' and is not marked finished. Signed out, the card shows only Next and Open on AO3. It is not modal and dismisses with a swipe down. Compose: the same content as an ElevatedCard in the reader's bottom-bar slot, with FilledTonalButton plus IconToggleButton(Icons.Filled.Favorite) and `performHapticFeedback(HapticFeedbackType.Confirm)`. ### visual-system

_The redesign has a strong set of parts (washes, kickers, rings, AO3 tiles) but no rules for using them together. Everything is tinted, uppercased or boxed at the same volume, and each screen picks its own accent, left edge, corner radius and header style, so nothing leads. That is why it looks good but not finished. Five rules would fix most of it: (1) three colour roles, each with one job: app accent for controls, fandom hue for identity only, AO3 legend colours only inside the AO3 signal glyphs; (2) one page gutter; (3) two surfaces only, a panel and a capsule chip, with corners that follow the device; (4) one uppercase label (the kicker), with every other header in sentence-case text styles; (5) every floating glass bar gets a scroll-edge effect, and every large title collapses into an inline one. The evidence set has no dark-mode capture, so dark mode is unverified._
