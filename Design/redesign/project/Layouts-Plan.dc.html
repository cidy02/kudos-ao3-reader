One layout per surface

Proposal, 2026-10-28. The Shelves / Ledger switch and the Detailed / Ledger / Compact menu go. Each surface gets the shape that suits it, as in Books and Music; only lists of everything keep one choice, List or Grid. No metadata is dropped anywhere.

Why

A switch that restyles a whole page makes every section the same shape: the “one-dimensional” feel the critique named.

Apple keeps a view choice only where you browse everything (Books › Library: List or Grid; Files). Curated pages are drawn once.

Summaries matter most where you choose what to read, so they lead in Search and Browse and step back in your own lists.

Long-press any work, anywhere: the preview shows its summary and tags, so nothing is out of reach.
