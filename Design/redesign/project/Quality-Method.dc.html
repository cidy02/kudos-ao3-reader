Reader Response: how Kudos judges quality

AO3 sorts by single counts, so old, long and serialized works win and new or short ones sink. Kudos ranks the works already loaded by figures every blurb carries, each compared with works of the same shape, and asks AO3 to do the narrowing it can do exactly. Pages load as the reader scrolls, or up to five at once when they tap Rank Top 100. Checked against AO3’s source and live searches on 28 October 2026; the full method and build plan are in notes/quality-signals-spec.md.

Run live: created_at date ranges, revised_at:>now-90d, otp:true, -series.title:* and hits/kudos ranges. Same syntax, not run: hits:>=1000, hits:[300 TO 5000], created_at:>now-6M. The proposal’s revised_at: >"3 months ago" fails with a syntax error. Ratio bands (kudos > x AND hits < y) are left out: they drop 5% works and keep 0.4% ones.

Rank Top 100 (owner approved, 28 Oct 2026). One tap loads pages 2 to 5 of the same search, strictly one after another, 3 seconds apart, cancellable, at most 10 such pages in 10 minutes. Each request adds feature=rank-top-100; reader-requested pages 2-5 to the KudosReader User-Agent, so AO3 can see which feature made it and reach the project through the linked repository. A 429, 403 or 503 ends the run; two throttled runs in a day switch it off for 24 hours.
