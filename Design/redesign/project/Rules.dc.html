Kudos design rules

The rules behind every board, from the direction you chose. Each one exists so a screen has a clear front and back: a calm canvas, one kind of surface on it, and colour that only appears when it means something.

Colour has a job

App colourEverything you tap (buttons, links, the tab bar, toolbars, selected chips, switches) and states about you: progress, kudos given, new chapters. One colour, AO3 red, fixed: there is no colour setting. On Dark and OLED, text and links take a lighter step of it (#FF7A70); filled controls keep #990000.
Fandom colourIdentity only: generated covers, the Resume card, the fandom label above a title, and the wash on a work’s or fandom’s own page. Never on a button, the tab bar or a toolbar: those stay AO3 red on every page.
Tag coloursAO3’s own palette, only inside the four tags (rating, category, warnings, status). Nowhere else.

Two backgrounds

Plain for content: Home, shelves, Work Detail, Search, Browse, the reader. Grouped for lists, settings, forms and sheets, with sections on it like the Settings app. No custom paper, texture or gradient behind the app.

Whose page is this?

A work’s or a fandom’s Work Detail, a fandom’s works, Author, Series: the wash, in that colour, does the job of a cover. It fades as you scroll and the large title shrinks into the bar.
Yours, or the app’s Home, Library, Account, queues, Settings, editors, sheets: no wash. On Home the Resume card carries the colour instead.

Shape and space

16 pt corner radius everywhere, capsules fully round. 16 pt edge on every screen. Two container styles:

Card
System grey on plain, white on grouped. The one gradient card per screen is your place in a work.

Let iOS do it

  • No empty bar row. Tabs (Home, Library, Browse, Account) put the large title on the toolbar row, as the app’s .inlineLarge does. Pushed pages use the small centred title in the bar (.inline), with extra actions in a ••• menu so it never gets crowded. A work, fandom or collection page starts its own heading straight under the bar, and that heading shrinks into it on scroll.
  • Liquid Glass only on navigation: the tab bar, toolbars, the Read Aloud accessory.
  • Sheets with grabbers, standard menus and context menus, swipe actions.
  • SF Symbols for every icon; Dynamic Type for every size.
  • No instructions written on the page (“Tap a tag to…”, “Swipe a row to…”): controls explain themselves. The exception is Settings and setup forms, where a short footer says what a choice does, as Apple’s Settings do.
  • “Tap to reveal” blur on mature works, everywhere a work appears.
  • Tab bar stays visible on pushed screens and minimises on scroll (HIG: tab bars); it hides only under modals, the reader and selection. Task screens like Comments use the minimised tab beside their bar. Today the app hides it on pushed screens (hidesFloatingTabBar): a code change to make.
  • Tab icons are filled symbols with one-word labels; Search is the separate button at the trailing end.
  • Back and Close are symbols, never the words; one prominent action (Done, Post, Add) sits trailing; extra actions go in ••• (HIG: toolbars).
  • Sheets: Close leading, confirm trailing; Reset lives in the form, not the bar (HIG: sheets).
  • Toolbar controls stay untinted glass; state shows in the symbol’s colour, not a filled button.
  • Title-style capitalisation on buttons, segments and menu items; at most five segments.
  • No A–Z index next to tappable row controls; favourites are a marker, set by swipe or menu (HIG: lists).
  • iPhone Duo: status bar, toolbar and tab bar on the trailing edge when closed or open in landscape, where split views divide at the fold (HIG: designing for iPhone Duo). Open in portrait is laid out like an iPad mini: horizontal bars, one full-height view, no split at the fold.

The parts

Resume card: your place in a work. Home uses this variant, Work Detail the one with the legend and Resume Chapter.
Generated covers: the fandom picks the colour family, the title picks the shade and where the letter and title sit, so a work always looks the same and two works never match. Tags under every cover.
Inline: Work Detail and heroes, one line.
Strip: under covers.
Grid: leads every row.
Labeled chips: the Symbols key only. Filters use pickers and checkable rows, as the app does.
