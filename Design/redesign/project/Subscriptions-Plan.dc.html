Within the networking policy

Checks run only while Kudos is open (Home or Subscriptions on screen), one request at a time through the existing request slots, and stop when you leave.

Each source is checked at most every 6 hours; tags are capped (proposed: 20 followed tags). Pull to refresh checks now, still one at a time.

Feeds are public: no login, no session cookie sent. A feed lists a tag’s newest works only, so a busy tag shows “and more” and links to its full page.

Open for you: notifications when the app is closed would need background checks, which the policy forbids today. AO3’s own emails still arrive as before.
