What changes

The hero’s picture is the passage where you stopped, in your reading font, with your place marked. Shelf covers are plain-title covers on the fandom’s paper colour; the title picks the shade and the layout, so each work keeps its own. What changes

Each work is drawn as its chapters: one bar per chapter, as tall as its word count, filled as far as you’ve read, dashed where the author hasn’t posted yet. A one-shot and a 30-chapter WIP can’t look alike, so a shelf stops being one shape repeated. What changes

Home stops being a stack of identical shelves: one large hero, square art for new chapters, a row grid led by AO3’s four-tag square, and queue rows with the segmented bar. Art is generated from the fandom and the title, like Music’s playlist art, and stays the same for that work. Work Detail reads like an album page: the chapters are the track list, with reading times, and the ones you’ve read are dimmed.
