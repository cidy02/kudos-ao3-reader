Motion and haptics

Proposed · not in today's app

Eight moments get their own motion. Everything else is left to iOS. Each tile loops a sketch of the movement; the timing, haptic and Reduce Motion version are under it.

Motion says where it went

Every animation starts at the thing you touched and ends at the thing it became. Nothing moves for decoration.

The page is still

In the reader nothing moves unless the reader moves it or asked for Read Aloud. No looping animation anywhere in the app.

A haptic means AO3 answered

Success when AO3 accepts something you sent. Error when it refuses. Selection ticks while dragging or scrubbing. Nothing else taps.

Three springs, system first

Snappy for controls. Smooth for navigation and progress. Bouncy for kudos and the new-chapter badge only. If iOS already animates it, use that.

Leave to iOS

Tab switches, pushes, sheets, menus, context-menu previews, swipe actions, pull to refresh, the keyboard, the large title shrinking into the bar, the tab bar minimising on scroll. No custom timing on any of them.

Never

Confetti. Shaking. Skeletons that shimmer for more than a second. Parallax on covers. Motion on the reader page. A haptic for something only the app did. Sound, apart from Read Aloud.

Settings the app follows

Reduce Motion: every card above has its version. System Haptics off: no haptics, nothing replaces them. Low Power Mode: the Resume bar and the badge appear without animating.
