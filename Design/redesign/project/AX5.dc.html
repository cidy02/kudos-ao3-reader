Largest text sizes

AX5, the largest accessibility size. Every style scales with Dynamic Type, so nothing here uses a fixed size; the layout changes shape instead of truncating.

  • Rows stack: the four-tag square sits on its own line above the title, then byline, stats and progress, each wrapping freely.
  • The four tags become a vertical list with their labels, one per line.
  • The Resume card drops the ring and chapter bars for a single progress bar, and its buttons stack full width.
  • Stats become label-over-value rows instead of a 3 × 3 grid.
  • Toolbar buttons grow to 56 pt; the large title wraps and still shrinks into the bar on scroll.
  • Generated covers on shelves give way to rows at AX sizes, as Apple Books does, so titles stay readable.
