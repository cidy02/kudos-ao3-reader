# Feature ideas for the owner (inspired by Apple's apps)

Ideas for Kudos **as a whole** (any platform, not only the iPhone Duo) that came from looking at how Apple's
own apps work during the Duo redesign (owner, 2026-10-24: *"if any apple apps give you inspiration for a
feature that you think should be in the app as a whole (meaning on any platform), do note it in a separate
file so i can review and approve or deny"*).

Nothing here is built until the owner approves it. Duo-only layout changes are not listed here: those are
Claude's decisions (owner, 2026-10-24 11:50) and are recorded in `DECISIONS.md`.

**How to answer:** change an idea's **Status** to `Approved` or `Denied` (a note is welcome), or just tell
Claude which numbers. Approved ideas join the Living Prompt's queue.

Each idea: what Kudos would do; the Apple app and behaviour it comes from; which platforms; why it helps a
reader; what it would cost (rough size, risks, AO3 traffic if any); and whether it changes stored data.

## 1. Find a setting by name

- **What Kudos would do:** a search field on the Settings page that finds any setting by its name or a
  related word ("font", "download", "blur") and opens it.
- **Inspired by:** the Settings app's search (iPhone, iPad, Mac).
- **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Android.
- **Why it helps a reader:** Kudos has many settings across Reading, Downloads, Library, Privacy and Backup;
  finding one shouldn't depend on remembering its category.
- **Cost:** small to medium (a few days per platform: a local index of setting labels, tests). No AO3 traffic.
  No stored data changes.
- **Status:** Proposed

## 2. A reading line guide

- **What Kudos would do:** an optional reading aid in the reader's options that dims the text above and below
  the line you're reading, so the eye keeps its place on long pages.
- **Inspired by:** Apple Books' Line Guide.
- **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, Android (macOS's older reader later).
- **Why it helps a reader:** long chapters and small text; helpful for readers with attention or tracking
  difficulties. Off by default.
- **Cost:** medium to large (one to two weeks: it must follow reflow, selection and accessibility in each
  reader). No AO3 traffic. One new reader setting stored.
- **Status:** Proposed

## 3. Back to where I jumped from

- **What Kudos would do:** after jumping elsewhere in a work (Contents, a search result, a bookmark, a note), a
  small "Back to page 12" button returns to the passage you left, as long as you haven't read on.
- **Inspired by:** Apple Books' return-to-previous-location control.
- **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, Android (macOS later).
- **Why it helps a reader:** checking a footnote, a tag or an earlier chapter no longer means hunting for your
  place afterwards.
- **Cost:** medium (three to five days per reader: a short history of jump origins, the button, tests). No AO3
  traffic. Nothing new stored beyond the session.
- **Status:** Proposed
