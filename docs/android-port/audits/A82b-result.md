# A82b: Second 35 Screens Side-by-Side Audit

## Counts

- **Match**: 13 (`authorabout`, `seriesedit`, `chsignupdetail`, `prompttags`, `tagset`, `writingpreview`, `chapters`, `edittags`, `tagseditor`, `seriespicker`, `collectionsgifts`, `creatorspicker`, `bulkedit`)
- **Minor**: 4 (`inboxfilter`, `authorworksfilter`, `tagseteditor`, `aboutkudos`)
- **Differs**: 18 (`seriesdetail`, `seriesreorder`, `seriesremove`, `colitems`, `colsfilter`, `chsettingsedit`, `chapterform`, `texteditor`, `recovery`, `parentpicker`, `bulktagstate`, `bulknames`, `prefhelp`, `privacydata`, `availability`, `pairing`, `revokedevice`, `backupimport`)
- **Capture problems**: 16 screens (`inboxfilter`, `authorabout`, `seriesdetail`, `seriesedit`, `seriesreorder`, `seriesremove`, `authorworksfilter`, `chsettingsedit`, `chsignupdetail`, `tagset`, `tagseteditor`, `edittags`, `bulkedit`, `bulktagstate`, `bulknames`, `prefhelp`)

## Differs

- **`seriesdetail`**: Light, Sepia, OLED, and largest-text columns, Android — omits the series bookmark count (`62`), `Restricted` lock badge, and updated date (`08 Jul 2026`) from the series summary card (and omits zero-count comment/kudos/bookmark/hit stats on work cards), while moving `STAR WARS` and `COMPLETE` to top pills and `by Avery Writes` to the bottom of the card.
- **`seriesreorder`**: Largest-text column (Sepia big) on iPhone truncates work titles to `A Lamp at the Crossi...` and `The Last...` while Android shows them in full; in Light, Sepia, and largest-text columns, Android omits the trailing drag-handle icons (`≡`) shown on each row on iPhone.
- **`seriesremove`**: Dark and largest-text columns, both apps — iPhone opens with `The Dawn Cycle · 0 works` and shows only the last-work deletion note (`AO3 deletes a series when its last work leaves...`), whereas Android opens with `The Dawn Cycle · 3 works` and lists the three works with remove buttons (`⊖`) and `A removed work stays posted and only leaves this series.`
- **`colitems`**: All columns, both apps — iPhone renders `Unrevealed` and `Anonymous` as read-only `Off` / `On` text (and truncates the bottom-left username to `AO3_Reader...` in Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED) while Android renders interactive toggle switches and appends `(Member)` to the username; iPhone also opens user-wide items (`Your works in AO3 collections` with 3 status chips) while Android opens collection-scoped items (`Winter Exchange` with a 4th `Approved` chip clipped at the right edge).
- **`colsfilter`**: Light, Sepia, and largest-text columns, iPhone — the pinned bottom bar behind `Reset` is drawn in bright white instead of matching the Sepia/sheet surface, making the disabled `Reset` button nearly invisible against the white bar.
- **`chsettingsedit`**: Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED columns, both apps — Android adds an `Assignments sent` (`Manual`) row in `SCHEDULE` not shown on iPhone, omits `4 sign-ups` from the subtitle, renders `Sign-up instructions` as a single card row without iPhone's `SIGN-UP INSTRUCTIONS` section header, and styles `Save changes` as a left-aligned list row instead of iPhone's pinned filled button.
- **`chapterform`**: Largest-text column (Sepia big), iPhone — in the `Position` row, `After chapter` is clipped to `After` (with `chapter` cut off below the card's bottom edge) while Android displays `After chapter` in full.
- **`texteditor`**: Largest-text column (Sepia big), iPhone — truncates `Recovery copy on this device · Save from the work form` to `...Save f...` (while Android wraps it in full) and collapses `Done` into a second `•••` icon; in Sepia and largest-text columns on iPhone the bottom formatting toolbar container is drawn in Light theme (white/grey) instead of Sepia, and in all columns Android shows a bold `Chapter text` title above the word-count kicker that iPhone omits.
- **`recovery`**: Sepia and largest-text columns, iPhone — sheet is drawn in Light theme (white) instead of Sepia; in largest-text (Sepia big), iPhone truncates the heading, paragraphs, and action labels (`Recover unfin...`, `was saved on t...`, `form has chan...`, `Restore local c...`, `Delete this loc...`) while Android wraps or displays them in full; and in all columns iPhone displays a second warning paragraph (`The text on the form has changed since this copy began...`) not shown on Android.
- **`parentpicker`**: Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED columns on iPhone truncate the `Language of the source` picker to `Please select a la...` (and largest-text on iPhone truncates the URL field to `https://archiv...`), while all columns on Android clip the right end of the `URL` field inside the `SOURCE WORK` card (`https://archiveofourown.org/w`).
- **`bulktagstate`**: Sepia and largest-text columns, iPhone — the options card is drawn in bright white (Light theme) instead of Sepia; in Light and OLED columns, iPhone displays only a compact nav-bar title and leading selection circles (`○`) on each row, whereas Android displays an `AO3 ACCOUNT / Archive warnings` header block and trailing `Unchanged >` labels without leading circles.
- **`bulknames`**: Sepia and largest-text columns, iPhone — the `Collection name` and `Add` rows are drawn in a full-width bright white (Light-theme) rectangle instead of a rounded Sepia card; in Sepia, Android also shows the `AO3 ACCOUNT / Add to collections` header block and both a left label and right placeholder (`Collection name   Collection name`) on the input row whereas iPhone shows only a compact nav title and single placeholder.
- **`prefhelp`**: Light, Sepia, OLED, and largest-text columns, iPhone — renders a redundant `Privacy Preferences` card with a trailing `?` badge below the `Privacy Preferences` sheet title (pushing `Allow others to invite me to be a co-creator.` and the FAQ footer below the fold), whereas Android shows the `Privacy Preferences` heading once and goes straight to the help cards.
- **`privacydata`**: Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED columns, Android — includes an `Imported fonts` (`12 KB`) row under `STORED ON THIS DEVICE` between `Original files kept` and `Draft recovery` that is not present on iPhone.
- **`availability`**: Largest-text column (Sepia big), iPhone — truncates the heading `Check Availability` to `Check Avail...` while Android displays `Check Availability` in full.
- **`pairing`**: Sepia and largest-text columns, iPhone — sheet background and cards are drawn in Light theme (grey/white) instead of Sepia; in all columns, iPhone includes a `Scan a QR Code` row (and leading row icons) whereas Android omits `Scan a QR Code` (noting `Kudos on Android never scans a code itself.`) and prints the raw hex key at the bottom.
- **`revokedevice`**: All columns, iPhone — truncates the sheet title (`Revoke Demo readi...` in Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED; `Revoke Demo...` in largest text) while Android wraps `Revoke Demo reading tablet?` in full; in Sepia and largest-text columns on iPhone, the `Reason` card is drawn in bright white (Light theme) instead of Sepia (and includes a `Reason` header row inside the card not on Android).
- **`backupimport`**: Sepia and largest-text columns, iPhone — the `This backup`, `Merge`, and `Replace Library...` cards are drawn in bright white (Light theme) instead of Sepia.

## Minor

- **`inboxfilter`**: Light and Sepia columns, iPhone — omits the `Inbox Filters` title from the sheet header bar that Android displays.
- **`authorworksfilter`**: Dark column, Android — adds a `Direction` sublabel above the `Ascending` / `Descending` segmented control inside the `Sort by` card that iPhone omits.
- **`tagseteditor`**: Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED columns, both apps — iPhone uses ALL-CAPS field labels inside each card (`FANDOM TAGS TO ADD`, etc.) and a centered filled `Save tags` button, whereas Android uses sentence-case labels (`Fandom tags to add`, etc.) and a left-aligned `Save tags` card row.
- **`aboutkudos`**: Dark, Light, Sepia, and OLED columns, iPhone — displays literal unparsed Markdown asterisks (`**GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**`) in the `License` card whereas Android renders the license name in bold.
