# 3dc: a collection and its Manage pages beside the iPhone's (Android)

Done 2026-10-12 by Claude, alone. Gate 2,632. Pictures: `shots/3dc/`. The first of the
Challenges screens put beside the iPhone's. Both demos open the same collection
(`winter_exchange`) with their own demo pages, so names and numbers differ; the pages'
parts were compared.

## The same

- **The collection's own page**: the title, its figures, Works / Bookmarks / People, the
  eight Manage rows in the same order, Recent with the ledger card, the pages.
- **Moderation, Prompts, Your sign-up, Challenge**: the same sections, rows and notes.

## Fixed

1. **Three pages' main action was a plain line of text.** iOS ends each of these pages
   with a plain button and a filled one: Create sign-up, New prompt, Submit sign-up, Claim
   a pinch hit. Android drew only the last filled; the other three were text beside text,
   and read as switched off. All four use one button now (`ChallengePrimaryAction`).
2. **Sign-ups and Assignments filtered with pills; iOS and Android's own Prompts use a
   segmented control.** Both use it now. At the large text sizes the pills stay, as three
   segments do not fit a row there.
3. **Edit collection labelled its sections as Settings does** ("Header", sentence case,
   at the body's size). iOS, and every other AO3 form on Android, uses the small capitals
   over a rule ("HEADER"). It does now.

Seen in Sepia beside the iPhone's, Sign-ups in Dark and at 2.0×. Tapped: the Unmatched
segment (the list narrowed), and Create sign-up (the sign-up form opened).

## Left

- An unmatched sign-up's "Open on AO3" on Assignments is a line of accent text; iOS's is a
  small pill with a compass.
- A sign-up's "Matched" badge sits at the row's far end on Android and after the name on
  iOS.
- Android names the maintainers under the collection's title; iOS does not.
