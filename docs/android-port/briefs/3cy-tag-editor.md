## Fixed

**The pills were drawn 48 dp tall**, the height they are touched by, where iOS's are the
height of their words. A pill is drawn at its own height inside the 48 dp it is held and
dragged by, and the rows sit closer. The same fault as 3cx's chips, in the other screen.

Seen at 1.0× beside the iPhone's and at 2.0×. A tag removed by its "×". A pill dragged
onto another on the emulator (`input draganddrop`): the order changed.
